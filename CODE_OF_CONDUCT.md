# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in the SteamLookup project a harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity,
gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

* Being respectful and welcoming to others
* Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
* Focusing on what is best for the project and community
* Showing empathy towards other community members

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

* Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments, in public or private
* Trolling or deliberately inflammatory behavior
* Publishing others' private information without their permission
* Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention
* Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these
standards, and will take fair and appropriate action in response to behavior
they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful. Maintainers may
remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other
contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within the project's GitHub repository, including
issues and pull requests, in the project's Discord server
(https://discord.gg/pJC8mqQAQC), and when an individual is representing the
project in public.

## Reporting

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported to the maintainer at
**panos.etos@gmail.com**, or by messaging a moderator in the Discord server.
All reports will be reviewed and handled promptly and
fairly, and the reporter's privacy will be respected.
